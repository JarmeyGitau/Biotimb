Common Questions

What is a bio septic system?

A bio septic system is an environmentally friendly wastewater treatment system that uses natural biological processes to break down waste.

What waste can it handle?

It can treat wastewater from toilets, bathrooms, kitchens and other domestic sources.

Does a biodigester smell?

A properly installed and maintained system should not produce unpleasant odours.

How long does installation take?

Installation time depends on system size, soil conditions and project requirements.

Does it require maintenance?

Yes. Regular inspection and proper usage help maintain the system performance.

Can it be installed in homes?

Yes. Bio septic systems are suitable for homes, apartments, schools and businesses.

Is it cheaper than a septic tank?

In many cases, biodigesters have lower long-term maintenance requirements compared to traditional septic systems.

Is it environmentally friendly?

Yes. It reduces pollution and supports sustainable wastewater management.
